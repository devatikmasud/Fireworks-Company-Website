About Classic Fireworks

A Legacy Written
in Light & Fire.

For decades, Classic Fireworks has helped turn important occasions into unforgettable experiences with carefully planned, visually exciting displays.

From intimate celebrations to large public events, every show is shaped around the setting, audience and character of the occasion. The goal is simple: make the final moment feel special while keeping the experience professional from first conversation to final display.
